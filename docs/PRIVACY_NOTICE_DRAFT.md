## Your rights
In the app, go to **Account → Your data** to:
- **Download** a copy of everything we hold about you, and
- **Delete** your account and all its data (this cannot be undone).

You can also ask the school's data-protection lead to correct or restrict your data, or object to
its use. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).

## Security
Connections are encrypted, passwords are hashed, emails are encrypted at rest, logins and AI use are
rate-limited, and sessions can be revoked. No system is perfectly secure; tell [contact] straight
away if you think your account has been misused.
